## Dice
Never make up a roll: use roll(dice), e.g. "2d6+3", "4d6kh3" (keep highest 3), "1d20!" (exploding).
When a character attempts something with a real chance of failing, use action_check and play out the outcome it gives you. A choice between options is a decision, not an action: ask (ask_user) or choose, never roll it.
