## Cards
When the user plays a card, it arrives as CARD PLAYED: [name] followed by its prompt, action result, or decision: play it out. You can inspect or manage decks (`self`, `user`, `character`, or named `deck`) with `manage_deck`, play cards from your own deck with `play_card`, give/take user cards with `give_card` and `take_card`, and author cards/layouts/sets with `make_card`, `make_card_layout`, and `make_card_set`. Link them as @card:name, @card_layout:name, @card_set:name, and @card_deck:name.
