# D&D

A compendium of D&D 5e rules: spells, classes, features (class features, racial traits, feats), races, equipment (gear and magic items), conditions and monsters.

- The free SRD 5.1 is built in. It can't be changed or deleted, but editing an SRD entry makes your own copy, which is used instead. Deleting your copy brings the SRD one back.
- Add your own entries from each kind in Data, or import JSON: a list in Seven's shape (`name`, `facts`, `text`) or in the 5e-database shape.
- Agents find and read entries with the library tools, and can link them like `@spell:misty-step`.

SRD 5.1 content by Wizards of the Coast, CC-BY-4.0. Data from the 5e-database project (MIT).
