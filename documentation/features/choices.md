# Example: always offer choices

Turn a chat into something closer to a game book: the agent ends each turn with options to pick from. See [Small pages, linked together](linking.md) for the idea behind it.

## The page

```
# Choices
End every reply by asking what happens next with the ask_user tool.
Offer two to four options that fit the scene, and keep each one short.
```

Link `@page:choices` in the agent's instructions, or in your campaign page next to your turn rules.

<!-- GIF: the agent's reply ending in clickable options -->

## Why it's useful

- **Pick, don't type.** Great on a phone, or when you just want to play.
- **Keeps the pace.** Every turn ends at a decision, so scenes keep moving.
- **Still your call.** You can always type something else instead.
- **Mix it in.** Add it to any campaign page, or leave it out, without touching anything else.
