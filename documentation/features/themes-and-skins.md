# Themes and skins: make it look like yours

Seven doesn't have to look like a cyberpunk terminal. Pick a different skin and the whole app changes: colours, fonts, shapes, frames, pictures, all of it.

<!-- GIF: cycling through the skins that come with Seven -->

## Themes and skins

- A **skin** is the whole look: colours, fonts, type, shapes, ornaments, pictures and skies.
- A **theme** picks a skin and adds your own touches on top: accent colours, background and effects.

Avatars, maps, backgrounds and effects all follow the skin's colours, so everything fits together.

## Make your own

Open the skin editor and change anything, with the whole app previewing live as you go. Colours, fonts (bring your own font files), shapes, ornaments, pictures: each has its own page, and you can see just what you've changed.

<!-- IMAGE: the skin editor -->

## Or have an agent make one

Skins are plain data, so an agent can write one for you. Describe the look you want, point the agent at [the skin guide](LINK-TO-SKIN-DOCS), and drop the result into Seven.

## Share them

Skins export and import like anything else in Seven, pictures and fonts included. A skin is only checked values, never code, so a skin someone shares with you can't run anything on your machine.
