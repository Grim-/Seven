# Example: let cards move the story

Builds on [the mood mechanic](stats-and-rules.md). See [Small pages, linked together](linking.md) for the idea behind it.

## Give the character more to feel

Make a few stats that explain themselves: **Anger**, **Fear** and **Trust**, each with a range and a description of what low and high mean. Put them in the same group, say `mind`.

Change the rules page to watch all of them:

```
Before every reply, look up your character sheet and read every stat
in your mind group. Let their current values shape how you speak and act.
```

## Let the cards push them

Gameplay cards can roll a check and apply effects on a hit or a miss. Make a few that move the mind stats:

- **Taunt**: on a hit, Anger goes up.
- **Ghost story**: on a hit, Fear goes up.
- **Shared drink**: on a hit, Trust goes up and Anger goes down.

Play them on the character and see what happens. You never tell the agent how to feel; the cards change the numbers and the agent reads them.

## Why it's fun

- **Surprising, but fair.** Rolls decide whether a card lands, so the scene can go either way.
- **Stats mix.** High Fear with high Trust plays very differently from high Fear with low Trust, and you didn't write either.
- **Add a stat whenever you like.** Put it in the mind group and the rules page already covers it.
