# Pages

A page is a wiki page, not a text file. It's the place for anything you or your agents should know: a shopping list, notes on a project, everything about a character, how you like your coffee.

## What a page holds
- **Text**: markdown with headings. Agents can read or write one section at a time, so a long page stays cheap to use.
- **Items**: a page can also be a list, a checklist or a table. You give it fields (text, number, yes/no, date, choice, tags, link, file) and it's a small database.
- **A picture**, shown with the page.
- **A voice**, so a page about a character can speak in scenes.
- **Tags** to group pages.

The same items can be viewed as a table, list, checklist, cards, board, calendar or chart, on the page itself or on a board.

## A shared wiki
- **One for everyone.** Every agent can find and read pages (within the access you give it), so something written once is known everywhere. Anything every agent should know belongs in a page.
- **Written by both sides.** You edit pages yourself; agents write them with tools. Neither has to copy-paste for the other.
- **Linked.** `@page:Shopping_list` links a page from anywhere: a chat, another page, a routine. Each page shows what links to it.
- **Shown anywhere.** A page, or just its items, can sit in the chat, the side panel, on a board or on Home.

## Pages that are more than pages
Some kinds are pages underneath, with a shape of their own: character sheets, repos and the kinds add-ons bring. They get everything above for free.

Some pages are generated each time they're read and can't be edited: "All …" for every kind, and days.

## Pages aren't
- Files. Documents, pictures and audio are files, kept as they are. A page can link to them or show them.
- An agent's memory. Memories are private to one agent; pages are shared.
