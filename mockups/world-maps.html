World maps · mockup

A map belongs to a world and is made of layers that pan and zoom as one. Places are the world's own records; their shapes are drawn on the map in Seven. Every piece below is its own component: PlaceTree, Viewport, LayerStack with TileLayer and ImageLayer, ShapeOverlay and ShapeEditor (also used by picture areas), LayerList, PlaceCard, ChangedBy, PresenceMarkers.

1 · Map screen

Left: the world's places as a tree, and the map's layers. Middle: the map; places are faint outlines that light up on hover, with who is there. Right: the selected place's card. Clicking a place in the tree or on the map selects it; a place with no shape on this map is marked in the tree.

The same editor as picture areas (ShapeEditor): drag a box or click points for an outline, drag to move, handles to resize. It edits the shape of the selected place on this map; the place itself stays the world record.
