Cards · mockup

Two kinds. Chat cards are a prebuilt prompt with a face: hover to read, click or drag into the chat, and its prompt is sent as your message. Map cards only work on the map: each has its button (Roll, Decide, Use), and pressing it does the thing. Either way the card glows and burns out, and one copy is used. Using the composer or playing a card is your choice; a card sends only its own prompt.

1 · The chat hand

Your character's chat cards (the agent's "Your character" setting) fanned along the bottom of the chat. Hover pulls one up big. Click a card (or drag it up into the chat) to play it. Try it.

2 · The map hand

In a world's Play view, your character's map cards sit under the map the same way. Each has its button. Roll opens the roll modal (and the check picker for "one check, picked"); Decide opens the decision; Use just happens. The card burns out, and the result goes to Play this out and the journey.

3 · Making a card

The card screen keeps its layout (how it looks) and gets Use: Chat card or Map card. A chat card has its prompt. A map card is an action, a decision or plain, with the action and decision forms you already have. Repos ({Wild magic surges}) roll and links resolve when it's played.

4 · On a character

The sheet's Cards section: every card they own with its count, chat and map cards apart. Add a card, Add a deck (adds its counts; the deck is untouched), right-click a card for Open, Take one away. The agent gives and takes with give_card / take_card (a card or a repo of cards) and never sees the hand.
