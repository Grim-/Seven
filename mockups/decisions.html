Actions and decisions · mockup

Two separate things, never mixed. An action is something attempted; checks and a roll pick the outcome, and nobody chooses it. A decision is a choice between options; nothing is rolled, and someone (you or an agent) picks. The words the agent gets always say which one it is, so it never has to guess whether to play out a result or ask.

1 · Rolling an action

Roll opens this over whatever you are on. The dice tumble, then land one check at a time, then the verdict and the outcome. Play this out starts a chat at the place with the result as your first message (it isn't there when you rolled from inside a chat: the result goes straight into that chat). When an agent rolls, the same card shows in its chat.
