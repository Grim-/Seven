Maps, Play and Edit · mockup

Steps 3–6 of the plan, together because they change the same screens and data. Maps become their own thing, made on their own screen. Worlds link to the maps they use and keep everything story: places and their shapes, characters and where they stand, actions, decisions. A world opens in Play; Edit shows the building tools. Layers become groups that show at chosen zooms. Objects are pictures placed freely on a map, and can link to things.

1 · The Maps screen

Every map, wherever it's used. A map belongs to no world; each card says which worlds use it. New map, or import from Tiled (drop a .tmj). Opening one goes to the map editor below.

Only the map: layers and objects. No places here; those are the world's. Click an object to select it and drag it; right-click it or empty map for its menu. A layer is a group: it holds tile maps, pictures and objects, and can show only between two zooms.

The world screen with Edit on: its maps as tabs (Use a map adds one from Maps; it's linked, not copied), the places tree, shapes drawn on the map (click, right-click, drag, as now), characters dragged to where they stand. Map layers and objects aren't editable here; Open in Maps goes to the map editor.

4 · The same world in Play

What you play in. The map is everything; building tools are gone. Your character at the top, the place you're at as a floating card with what's happening, who's here, and what you can do (actions and decisions as big buttons), Continue here to carry on the chat there. Click a place to look at it; click an object (the cellar door) for its chat, actions and decisions. Your character's token is dragged to move (step 7). The journey strip comes with step 7.
